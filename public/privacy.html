Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

ApnaBot ("we", "us", or "our") provides a platform that helps businesses manage customer conversations, orders, and notifications through WhatsApp Business messaging and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and business details provided when you sign up.
  • WhatsApp Business data: WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID, phone number ID, and message content exchanged with your customers, obtained through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Embedded Signup flow.
  • Customer and order data: information your business stores about its customers, products, and orders within the Service.
  • Usage data: log data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used to maintain and improve the Service.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including sending and receiving WhatsApp messages on your behalf.
  • To authenticate your account and connect your WhatsApp Business number via Meta's Embedded Signup.
  • To communicate with you about updates, support, and service-related notices.
  • To monitor, secure, and improve the Service and troubleshoot issues.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With Meta Platforms, Inc., as required to provide WhatsApp Business API connectivity.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf (e.g., hosting, cloud storage) under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of ApnaBot, our users, or others.

Data Retention

We retain account and message data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below. You may also disconnect your WhatsApp Business account from the Service at any time.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
