Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: August 9, 2026

ApnaBot ("we", "us", or "our") allows businesses and their customers to request deletion of personal data we hold. This page explains what data we store and how to request its deletion.

What Data We Store

  • Business account data: business name, contact details, business type, and connected WhatsApp Business Account information.
  • Customer data: WhatsApp phone numbers, message history, and booking details associated with a connected business.
  • Message content: messages exchanged between a business and its customers through the Service.

How to Request Deletion

If You Are a Business Using ApnaBot

You can request deletion of your business account and all associated data (customers, messages, bookings, and connected WhatsApp Business Account information) by:

  1. Emailing us at info@averixsolutions.co.in from your registered account email, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", or
  2. Contacting our support team through the ApnaBot app.

We will verify your identity as the account owner and process the deletion within 30 days. Deleting your account will also disconnect your WhatsApp Business Account from the Service.

If You Are a Customer of a Business Using ApnaBot

If you have messaged a business that uses ApnaBot and would like your data (message history, phone number, booking details) deleted, you may:

  1. Contact the business directly, as they control the data collected through their WhatsApp number, or
  2. Email us at info@averixsolutions.co.in with the phone number and business name involved, and we will forward your request to the relevant business and assist with removing the data from our systems.

Data Deletion via Facebook/Meta

If you have connected your WhatsApp Business Account to ApnaBot through Meta's Embedded Signup and wish to revoke that connection, you can also do so from your Meta Business Settings, in addition to contacting us directly using the methods above.

Timeframe

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, after which it is deleted.
